Digital Chains: The Rise of Internet Slavery

Written and Curated by Jessa Acosta

1. Introduction: The Invisible Chains

In the modern era, the internet is essential. However, the concept of Internet Slavery highlights a new crisis: the loss of psychological autonomy. When we scroll mindlessly for hours, we are no longer using the tool; the tool is using us.

2. Dopamine Engineering

Platforms are designed to trigger dopamine, the brain's reward chemical. Features like "Infinite Scroll" and notifications act like digital slot machines, keeping us trapped in a cycle of dependency.

Academic Glossary

Autonomy: The ability to make independent choices.
